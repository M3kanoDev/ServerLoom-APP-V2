# Security policy

Security matters to us. ServerLoom App runs a live hosting business, so thank you for helping keep it safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Instead, report privately in one of these ways:

1. Through GitHub's **[private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new)** on this repository
2. Through the contact form at **[iloomx.com](https://iloomx.com)**, with the subject "Security - ServerLoom"

Please include:
- what you found and where (URL or page)
- the steps to reproduce it
- the impact you believe it has

We aim to reply within **48 hours** and keep you updated until the issue is fixed.

## Scope

- `serverloom.com` and its customer panel
- the ServerLoom sign-in, checkout and billing flows

Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, spam, social engineering, and attacks on other customers' services.
Please test only against your own account and never access other people's data.
